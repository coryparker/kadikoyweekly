About.

I started as a writer at Nicki Swift and moved into editing. Over four years at Static Media, I worked across entertainment, current events, food and travel coverage, often handling 10 to 12 stories in a day.

I lived in Istanbul for seven years and organized a recurring community event in Kadıköy. Travel through more than 60 countries has given me a long list of stories I want to tell, especially about places that rarely fit the usual shorthand.

I’m open to editorial roles and writing assignments.
